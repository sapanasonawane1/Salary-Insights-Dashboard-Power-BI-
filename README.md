💼 Salary Insights Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing job market salary trends across industries, job roles, categories, and locations — built to help understand where the highest-paying opportunities are and what drives salary variation.

📌 Objective

Explore the job market to answer questions like: which industries and roles pay the most, how salary relates to experience required, and how job availability is distributed across categories and locations.

📊 What's in the Dashboard

KPIs: Total Jobs, Total Companies, Median Salary, Max Salary
Salary vs. Experience: Average salary against experience required (combo chart)
Industry breakdown: Average salary by Industry/Domain (pie chart)
Role breakdown: Average salary by Job Role (bar chart)
Job distribution: Total jobs by Job Category (donut chart)
Filters: Location, Industry/Domain, Job Category slicers

🛠️ Tools
Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query 

🧮 Dataset
Dataset Name : India_Job_Market_Dataset
Size : Rows : 601
       Columns : 15
Key fields: Job Role, Job Category, Industry/Domain, Location, Experience Required, Avg Salary, Median Salary, Max Salary, Total Jobs, Total Companies

💡 Key Insights

Education Industry offers the highest average salary, ahead of Tech industry by 2.4%.
Salary rises with experience required, with the steepest jump around 12+ years.
Technical accounts for the largest share of total job postings.
Bengaluru has the highest concentration of high-paying roles followed by Hyderabad.

▶️ How to Use
Download Dashboard1_Job_market.pbix
Open it in Power BI Desktop (free)
Use the Location, Industry/Domain, and Job Category slicers to filter and explore
